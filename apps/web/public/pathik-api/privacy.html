pathik Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · App: pathik: ট্রেনের সময়সূচি BD (Android com.hyelabs.bdraillive) · Developer: Pickixo

pathik shows Bangladesh train schedules, off days and the estimated position of a train, and has an AI helper called RailBot. It is free and shows ads. It is an independent app and is not run by Bangladesh Railway. This policy explains what information the app uses, who it is shared with, and why.

Information the app collects

  • Location (optional). If you allow location access, the app uses your precise location for its location features. You can use the app without it, and you can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • How you use the app. Which screens and features you use, so we can see what works and improve it.
  • Diagnostics. Crash reports and performance data, so we can fix problems.

RailBot (AI helper)

When you ask RailBot a question, the text you type is sent to an AI service provider so it can write the answer. Please do not type personal details such as your phone number, NID or ticket PNR into RailBot.

Ads

The app shows ads from an advertising network. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the network may receive:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your approximate location, for example from your IP address;
  • app interactions and diagnostic data.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).

Who data is shared with

  • Analytics and advertising providers, for the purposes above.
  • The AI service provider that answers RailBot questions.

All data is sent over an encrypted connection. We do not sell your data.

SMS tracking

If you use SMS tracking, an SMS goes to Bangladesh Railway's SMS service. Your mobile operator may charge you for it.

What we do not collect

  • The app has no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or photos.
  • The app does not sell tickets and handles no payments.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Keeping and deleting data

We do not keep a profile of you. Analytics, diagnostic and ad data is held by the providers above under their own retention rules. Uninstalling the app stops all collection. For questions about your data, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
