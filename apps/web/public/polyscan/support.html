Polyscan Support

Polyscan scans documents, reads the text in 100+ languages on your phone, and makes searchable PDFs. No account, no subscription, no scan limit. Free with ads; pay once to remove them.

Contact

Questions, problems or feature requests: support@pickixo.com. Please include your phone model, the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and, if you can, the language of the document.

Common questions

Is Polyscan free?
Yes. Every feature and every language is free, with no scan limit and no watermark. The free version shows ads.

How do I remove the ads?
Settings › Remove ads. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

I bought “Remove ads” on another phone. How do I get it back?
Open Settings › Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID (or Google account).

How do I scan?
Tap Scan and hold the phone over the page; the camera finds the edges and straightens the page. You can also import pictures from Photos or Files.

How do I add a language?
Settings › Text languages, then download the language. After that it works offline.

Why is some text wrong?

  • Use good, even light and hold the phone flat over the page.
  • Pick the right language (or languages) before reading the text.
  • Printed text works best; handwriting is not read reliably.

Which formats can I share?
Searchable PDF, Word (.docx), plain text and images (.jpg).

Privacy

Your scans and their text stay on your phone and are never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.
