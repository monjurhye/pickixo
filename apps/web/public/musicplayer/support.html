Pickixo Music Player Support

Offline music player with a 10-band equalizer for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Contact: support@pickixo.com

Adding music

  • iPhone and iPad: Library › + › Import Files… or Import Folder… to copy songs from the Files app, iCloud Drive or other storage providers. Folders keep their structure.
  • You can also open the Files app › On My iPhone/iPad › Music Player and drop songs there, or share an audio file from another app to Music Player.
  • From a Mac to iPhone/iPad: connect the device, open Finder, select the device › Files, and drag songs onto Music Player.
  • Mac app: drag songs or folders into the window, or use Add Music › Import.
  • If new songs don’t appear, open Settings › Rescan Library.

Supported formats

MP3, AAC, M4A (AAC and Apple Lossless), WAV, AIFF, CAF and FLAC. DRM-protected songs bought from other stores cannot be played.

Equalizer

  • Equalizer tab: switch it on, pick one of 14 presets or move the 10 band sliders (32 Hz to 16 kHz, ±12 dB). Double-tap a slider to reset it to 0 dB.
  • Use Preamp to lower the overall level if a boosted preset sounds distorted.
  • Save your own presets from the ••• menu.

Gapless playback

Songs with the same sample rate and channel count play back to back without a gap. You can turn this off in Settings › Gapless Playback.

Free trial and Pro

  • Start the free 7-day trial from the purchase screen to use every feature. Nothing is charged, and there is no subscription.
  • After 7 days, your library, imports and equalizer settings stay, and playing music needs Music Player Pro, a one-time purchase.
  • Pro works on all your iPhones, iPads and Macs signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Restore: Settings › Restore Purchases (also on the purchase screen).
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

No account, no tracking, no data collected. Read the privacy policy.

Contact

Email support@pickixo.com with your device model, OS version and app version (Settings › Version). We usually answer within two business days.
