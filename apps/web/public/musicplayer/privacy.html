Pickixo Music Player Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · App: Pickixo Music Player (iPhone, iPad and Mac, com.pickixo.musicplayer) · Developer: Pickixo

Pickixo Music Player plays the music files you already own, completely offline. It has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics.

We collect no data

  • Pickixo does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The App Store privacy label for this app is “Data Not Collected”.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not use the advertising identifier.
  • The app does not connect to any Pickixo server. The only network traffic is the App Store handling purchases.

Your music stays on your device

  • Songs you import are copied into the app’s own storage on your device (on iPhone and iPad, the Music Player folder you can see in the Files app). They are never uploaded.
  • The app reads tags such as title, artist, album and cover art from your files on the device to build your library.
  • Your library index, equalizer settings and presets are saved on your device only.
  • The app only opens the files and folders you choose to import (or drag in on a Mac). It does not scan the rest of your device.
  • Deleting a song in the app deletes that file. Deleting the app deletes all its files and settings.

Purchases

The free 7-day trial and the one-time “Music Player Pro” purchase are handled by Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app never sees your payment details and only learns whether the trial or Pro was obtained, and when the trial started. There is no subscription and nothing is charged automatically.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@pickixo.com. See also Pickixo Music Player Support.
