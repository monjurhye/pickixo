Survival Tiger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · App: Survival Tiger: Bagha Char (Android com.pickixo.survivaltiger) · Developer: Pickixo

Survival Tiger is a battle royale game. It is free, shows ads and has no real-money purchases. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information kept on your phone

  • Your settings, coins, cosmetics and match statistics are saved only on your device.
  • They are not sent to us. We run no accounts or servers for the game.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and share data from your device, such as:

  • the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic and performance data.

Google uses this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads are requested.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Google, then Ads.
  • Watching an ad for coins, or to get back up after being knocked out, is always your choice. Other full-screen ads appear only after a match has ended.

Local network play

When you play with a friend on the same Wi-Fi network, your device exchanges game data and its local network address directly with the other device. This does not pass through our servers, and we do not store it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • The game has no chat and no user accounts.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data deletion

We hold no data about you on any server. Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored on your device. For data collected by Google for ads, see Google's privacy controls linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
