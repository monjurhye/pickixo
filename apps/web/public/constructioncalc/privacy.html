ConstructionCalc Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · App: ConstructionCalc (Android and iOS com.pickixo.constructioncalc) · Developer: Pickixo

ConstructionCalc is a feet-inch-fraction calculator for carpenters, contractors and DIY users. The free version shows a banner ad; a one-time Pro purchase removes it. There is no account, and your calculations never leave your device. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information kept on your device

  • Your calculation history, settings (precision, units, theme) and whether you own Pro are saved only on your device.
  • They are not sent to us. We run no accounts or servers for the app.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. On Android they may be included in your own Google device backup if backup is turned on.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and share data from your device, such as:

  • the advertising ID (Android advertising ID, or the iOS IDFA if you allow tracking) and app set ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance data.

This data is sent encrypted. Google uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent tool. Where privacy law requires it (including some US states), you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not used and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Google, then Ads (or Privacy › Ads).
  • Buying Pro removes ads completely.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play or the Apple App Store. They process the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only asks the store whether Pro is owned on your account, so it can restore it.

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet and network state: loading ads and checking your Pro purchase. The calculator itself works offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google's ad SDK as described above.
  • Billing: buying and restoring Pro through Google Play.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

Website calculator

The web version of the calculator keeps its settings and history only in your browser's local storage.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not sell your data, and we share none ourselves; the only data sharing is what Google's ad SDK does, as described above.
  • The app has no user accounts and no analytics of our own.

Children

ConstructionCalc is a tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data deletion

We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device. For data collected by Google for ads, use Google's privacy controls linked above or your device's ad settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
