ConstructionCalc Support

ConstructionCalc adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides tape measurements in feet, inches and fractions, exactly. It also has concrete, stairs, roof and rafter, right-triangle and board-feet tools. Free with a banner ad; pay once for Pro.

Contact

Questions, problems or feature requests: support@pickixo.com. Please include your phone model and the app version (shown in Settings), and the calculation you typed if a result looks wrong.

Common questions

How do I type 5′ 3 ½″?
Tap 5 ft 3 in 1 / 2. Then an operator, the next measurement and =.

How do I change the fraction precision or units?
Tap the precision button (for example 1/16) above the keypad, or ⇄ to switch between feet-inch, inches and metric.

What does Pro include?
Stairs, Roof and rafter, memory keys (M+, M−, MR), unlimited history, and no ads. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?
Open Settings › Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID (or Google account).

Does it work offline?
Yes. Every calculation and tool works without internet. Only the ad and the purchase check need a connection.
