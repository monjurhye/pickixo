PlanTally Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026 · App: PlanTally: PDF Takeoff Measure (iPhone, iPad and Mac, com.pickixo.plantally) · Developer: Pickixo

PlanTally is made by Pickixo. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use PlanTally on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

We do not collect any data

PlanTally has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive your drawings, measurements, estimates, company details or any other content you create.

Where your data is stored

Everything you create in PlanTally is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync in PlanTally's Settings, your projects are also stored in your own private iCloud account, managed by Apple, so they appear on your other devices. We cannot see or access that data. You can turn iCloud sync off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

Purchases (the free 7-day trial and PlanTally Pro Lifetime) are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Scale reading on the sheet

When PlanTally reads the scale printed on a drawing, the text is recognized on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to any other service.

Files you share

When you export a report, spreadsheet or marked-up PDF, the file goes only where you choose to share or save it.

Children

PlanTally is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
