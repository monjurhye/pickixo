PlanTally Support

PlanTally measures construction drawings (PDF) to scale and turns the quantities into a priced bid, on iPad, iPhone and Mac. Try every feature free for 7 days; PlanTally Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase.

Contact

Need help? Email support@pickixo.com. We usually reply within two business days. Please include your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac), its software version, and what you were doing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start?
Tap + to create a project, then import a PDF from Files, iCloud Drive or the share sheet. Or tap Try Sample Project on the empty project list.

How do I set the scale?
Open a sheet and tap Scale. Pick an architectural, engineering or metric preset, or choose Calibrate with a Known Distance and mark a dimension you know. If the sheet has a printed scale, PlanTally offers it: tap Use to apply it.

What does the free trial include?
Every feature for 7 days. After it ends you can still open and view your projects; new measurements, editing and exports need PlanTally Pro Lifetime.

Is PlanTally Pro a subscription?
No. PlanTally Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase that works on your iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Settings › Restore Purchases (also on the purchase screen).

How do I move projects between devices?
Turn on iCloud Sync in Settings on each device, signed in to the same iCloud account.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

PlanTally collects no data. Your projects stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.
